## Ramen (ラーメン)

Most ramen are built on house tonkotsu (pork bone) broth simmered for many hours.

- **Tan Tan Ramen** — $15.95. House peanut and spicy sesame broth with spicy ground pork, peanuts, green onion and nori. The local favourite and top seller. _(signature · spicy · contains: peanut, sesame)_
- **Tonkotsu Ramen** — $15.95. The heart of all our ramen — pork bone broth simmered for hours until hearty and creamy. _(signature)_
- **Black Garlic Ramen** — $16.95. Tonkotsu with chashu, marinated bamboo, sweet corn, garlic chips, red beni shoga and shredded green onion, drizzled with black garlic oil. _(popular)_
- **Spicy Garlic Miso Ramen** — $15.95. A blend of spices gives traditional miso a savoury kick. _(spicy)_
- **Miso Ramen** — $15.95. Tonkotsu soup base with homemade miso paste.
- **Curry Ramen** — $15.95. Japanese curry blended into tonkotsu soup. _(mild spice)_
- **Shoyu Ramen** — $15.95. Soy-seasoned and one of our lightest soups — tangy, salty, savoury, with a marinated egg.
- **Shio Ramen** — $15.95. Tonkotsu seasoned with house sea salt: balanced pork flavour, light creamy broth, half egg.
- **Vegetarian Ramen** — $14.95. Vegetable broth with a generous portion of corn, bamboo, kikurage mushroom, seasonal veggies and three pieces of fried tofu. _(vegetarian)_
